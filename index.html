Better software for the age of AI

We study how developers and AI tools build software together, and how to keep the open-source code they rely on secure. We work with Thai software companies and partner labs abroad, and our students turn this research into tools and papers at international conferences.

Research

We study real software projects and the people who build them. Many of our results become tools that developers can use, such as Teddy, V-Achilles, and DepSecLens.

  • AI-assisted software development

    How developers work with AI coding tools and agents, and how far their output can be trusted. Recent studies cover the Agent Package Manager (APSEC 2026), LLM-powered code review (APSEC 2025), and AI projects on GitHub (ICSME 2025).

    AI agentsLLMscode review

  • Software security

    How open-source projects report and handle vulnerabilities, how developers can pick safer dependencies, and how to test systems for security problems. This line includes the SECURITY.md study (EMSE 2026) and DepSecLens, which received an Honorable Mention at NSC 2026.

    dependenciesvulnerabilitiessecurity testing

  • Software process and the Thai software industry

    How software companies, especially small firms in Thailand, plan, build, and improve their software, from ISO/IEC 29110 to automated tools and remote work. Recent results appear in JSS 2026, EMSE 2024, and InCIT 2025.

    ISO/IEC 29110Thai SMEsprocess improvement

  • Software analytics

    What data from software projects reveals about code, developers, and teams, such as how sprints unfold (Sprint2Vec, TSE 2025), how widely knowledge is held in a team (DEV-EYE, APSEC 2024), and how coding skill grows over time (PyGress, ASE 2025).

    mining repositoriesteamscode proficiency

  • Privacy in software

    How privacy requirements appear in real projects, and how to find them automatically. Work includes a taxonomy of privacy requirements in issue reports (IST 2023) and a study of automated ways to classify them (Automated Software Engineering journal, 2023).

    privacyrequirementsissue reports

  • Applied AI and digital health

    AI and software for health care and society, from dental image analysis and diabetic foot risk to home isolation platforms. Work includes explanations for a dental AI model (IEEE Access 2025) and a review of explainable AI and human trust (IEEE Access 2025).

    health careexplainable AIdental AI

Where students do real research

Since 2023, more than 30 of our undergraduates have spent a summer at partner labs abroad, and their projects have become papers at ICSME, APSEC, SANER, and ASE. Our students build tools that developers use, and their work has won awards at ICSME and Thailand's National Software Contest.
